What we do

Four practice areas, one architect across all of them.

Most Fusion problems cross the functional–technical line. We cover both sides, so a configuration decision and the data it produces are made by the same person.

Implementation & Support

End-to-end functional and technical lifecycle management across Fusion modules — from requirements through go-live and into steady-state support.

  • — Requirements, CRP cycles, UAT and cutover
  • — FBDI, ADFdi and HDL data migration
  • — L2/L3 support and quarterly-update readiness

Integration Architecture

Integration flows built to survive production volume, partial failures and reprocessing — not just the happy path.

  • — REST and SOAP APIs with retry and fault handling
  • — PL/SQL staging packages and database procedures
